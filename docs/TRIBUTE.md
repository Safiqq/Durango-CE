## In Appreciation

When official live service for *Durango: Wild Lands* ended in December 2019, the world of Durango—its vibrant ecosystems, prehistoric survival mechanics, dinosaur companions, and community clan islands—faced permanent loss.

The survival of Durango today as an accessible, playable experience is owed directly to the relentless passion, reverse-engineering talent, and open-source contributions of key pioneers in the community. 

We dedicate this tribute to:

### 1. SoloCode228
* **Repository**: [`SoloCode228/Durango-CustomServer`](https://github.com/SoloCode228/Durango-CustomServer)
* **Contributions**:
  * Pioneered foundational custom server architecture and reverse-engineering of Durango's custom packet protocol and MsgPack serializations.
  * Researched game cluster state machines, session management, world coordinates, and packet routing.
  * Established the bedrock upon which all modern Durango server emulation projects are built.

### 2. ShuuuuShi
* **Profile**: [ShuuuuShi on GitHub](https://github.com/ShuuuuShi)
* **Contributions**:
  * Developed foundational client-side redirection hooks that allowed the game client to interface with custom server endpoints.
  * Preserved key v3.3.6 client assets and runtime binaries that made decompilation and local restoration possible.
  * Kept the flame of Durango alive during the years following official shutdown.

## Preservation Mission

**Durango Community Contributors** are building upon this collective groundwork to deliver:
* **Fully Decompiled C# Source**: Transparent, verifiable, and free of proprietary telemetry or unneeded sockets.
* **Dual-Mode Gameplay**: Seamless support for both fully offline single-player exploration and community multiplayer servers.
* **Open Archival Standard**: Clean documentation, reproducible build toolchains, and strict security isolation practices.

Thank you, **SoloCode228** and **ShuuuuShi**, for your indispensable contributions to the Durango preservation movement.

*— Durango Community Contributors*
